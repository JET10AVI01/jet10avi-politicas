1. Introduction

JET10AVI respects the privacy of users of Loan Calculator. This Privacy Policy explains what information is used, how it is processed, and what happens when users voluntarily share calculation results.

The current version of the application is designed to operate locally on the user's device, without requiring an account or connecting to JET10AVI's own servers.

2. Information Used by the Application

To perform calculations, the application uses information voluntarily entered by the user, such as:

  • Loan amount.
  • Annual interest rate.
  • Loan term.
  • Recurring or one-time extra payments.
  • Selected currency.

This information is processed locally on the device to calculate installments, interest, balances, and amortization results.

JET10AVI does not collect or receive this loan information through its own servers.

3. Data Collection and Storage

The current version:

  • Does not require registration or account creation.
  • Does not intentionally collect personal information to send to JET10AVI.
  • Does not store loan calculations on JET10AVI's external servers.
  • Does not include advertising.
  • Does not include JET10AVI-operated analytics or user-tracking tools.

The entered values are used while the application is running. The current version does not provide a feature for saving loans to an account or online service.

4. Sharing Results

The application allows users to voluntarily share a loan summary through the device's sharing feature.

When users choose this feature, the summary may include the loan amount, interest rate, term, monthly installment, and calculated totals.

Users decide whether to share the information and which application or recipient to use. Once information is sent to another application or service, its handling is subject to that third party's privacy policies and practices.

JET10AVI does not control or take responsibility for how third-party applications or services subsequently process information shared by the user.

5. Device Permissions

The application uses only the technical capabilities necessary to provide its features.

The sharing feature uses the operating system's sharing mechanisms and the corresponding technical components to display available sharing options.

The current version does not need camera, microphone, contacts, or location permissions to perform calculations.

6. Third-Party Services

The application uses software components required for its operation, including tools for displaying charts and sharing results.

The current version does not integrate advertising services, analytics platforms, or JET10AVI-operated services that transmit data to its own servers.

If future versions introduce third-party services that collect or process information, their practices, the data involved, required permissions, and applicable disclosure and consent obligations will be reviewed.

7. Security

Because calculations are processed locally and are not sent to JET10AVI's own servers, exposure of information entered into the application is reduced.

However, no system or device can guarantee absolute security. Users are encouraged to keep their operating systems updated and protect access to their devices.

8. Children's Privacy

The application is a general financial calculation tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

9. Future Changes and Advertising

JET10AVI may update or expand the application's features. Future versions may include advertising, analytics services, new features, or integrations with external service providers.

The current version of the application does not contain advertisements or advertising tools.

Before introducing any service involving new data-processing practices, JET10AVI will review SDKs, permissions, privacy disclosures, Google Play declarations, and applicable legal requirements.

Material changes will be communicated clearly and in a timely manner. Where required, users will be asked for their explicit acceptance before the new data processing takes effect.

10. Updates to This Policy

This policy will be identified by a version number and an effective date. JET10AVI will retain a history of previous versions.

Minor changes that do not materially affect users' rights or the processing of their data may be communicated through available informational channels.

Material changes, particularly those involving new data categories, purposes, third-party recipients, or permissions, will be clearly communicated, and renewed acceptance will be requested where appropriate.
